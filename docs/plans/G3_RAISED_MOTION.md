| --- | --- | --- |
| --single-left-negative | -5≤L≤-1，R=0 | (200,200) → (-5,0) |
| --single-right-negative | L=0，-5≤R≤-1 | (1800,200) → (0,-5) |
| --dual-positive | 1≤L=R≤5 | (1000,1800) → (5,5) |
